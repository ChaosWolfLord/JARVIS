JARVIS, in your pocket.

The Android app for JARVIS, the projected AI whiteboard that runs on your own PC. Ask it by voice or text over your home Wi-Fi, get its alerts, and set reminders that ring even when the PC is off.

Download for Android All versions

Android 10 or newer · free

Install it in a minute

  1. Download the file with the button above, on your phone. Android may ask whether you trust Chrome to install apps: say yes (you can switch it off again afterwards).
  2. Open the downloaded file and press Install. If Google Play Protect says the app is unknown, that is because it is not from the Play Store; choose Install anyway.
  3. Connect it to your PC. Start JARVIS on the PC, open the status menu and look under Phone for the address and the fingerprint. Type the address in the app, check that the fingerprint matches, then enter the six-digit code from Show pairing code.

The phone and the PC need to be on the same Wi-Fi. Updates: the app looks for new versions by itself and offers them, then you open the downloaded file once to install. Your pairing and reminders stay.

What it does

Talk or type

Ask JARVIS anything from the sofa. Answers can show up on the wall, be read out on the PC speakers, or stay on the phone.

Reminders that always ring

Reminders are kept on the phone with their own alarms, so they go off even when the PC is off or out of reach.

Alerts when the app is closed

Optionally, finished timers and system problems from the PC reach you as notifications.

Works without the PC

Away from home? With your own AI keys copied to the phone, JARVIS still answers questions, searches the web and sets reminders.

Private by design

Home Wi-Fi only

The app only talks to your own PC, and your PC only accepts devices on your home network.

Pinned to your PC

On first run you compare a fingerprint with the one on the PC. After that the app trusts that key and nothing else.

Nothing goes to us

There is no account and no server of ours. The one thing the app asks the internet for is whether a newer version exists on GitHub, and you can switch that off in its settings.

You can check the download: its SHA-256 fingerprint is in each release's notes on the releases page, and the whole app is open on GitHub to read.
